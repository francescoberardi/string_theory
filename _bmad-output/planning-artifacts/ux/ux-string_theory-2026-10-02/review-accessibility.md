# Accessibility Review — string_theory

Reviewed: `DESIGN.md` and `EXPERIENCE.md` (both updated 2026-10-24), with the shipped `court_midnight_theme.dart` and the PRD as context. Target: WCAG 2.2 AA, Android (TalkBack, font scale, touch targets) and Flutter web (keyboard, semantics).

## Overall verdict

The palette is mostly sound. White, secondary text, Volt and the three health colors all clear AA on every surface, and the spines already commit to 48dp targets, text tier labels, a gauge semantics label and a Cancel-first destructive dialog. Two token choices fail AA on core paths, though. `text-tertiary` is used for the sync-pending text and hints at 2.7–3.4:1, and white on `health-critical` gives the Delete Account button 3.76:1. Several web and screen-reader behaviours are also unspecified or non-compliant: the focus indicator measures 1.4–1.7:1, the bare `L`/`N` shortcuts fail 2.1.4, critical failures show only in a 4 s snackbar, and Flutter web semantics are off by default. Both spines need a revision pass before stories are cut.

## Findings

### Critical

- **[critical]** `text-tertiary` (#606B70) carries load-bearing 12px text and fails 1.4.3 on every surface (2.72–3.35:1). That text is the `Waiting to sync` marker on cards (Flow 1), the bag sync summary, hints/placeholders in the Add Racquet form (Flow 2) and unit labels. The Do/Don't "the pending marker is text in `text-tertiary`" locks the failure in, and the shipped `bodySmall` uses `textTertiary` too. (DESIGN.md § Colors table, frontmatter `components.sync-pending-marker.text`, § Components → Racquet Card "Sync pending" and Bag Sync Summary, § Do's and Don'ts last bullet). *Fix (in palette):* switch `sync-pending-marker.text`, the Bag Sync Summary, hints and unit labels to `text-secondary` (6.25–7.71:1). Narrow `text-tertiary`'s use to "disabled controls and purely decorative glyphs only", and update the Don't to "the pending marker is text in `text-secondary`, never a color change". *Fix (token change):* raise `text-tertiary` to #8A969B (≥ 4.90:1 on all four surfaces) if a third text tier must stay distinct. This also changes the shipped `bodySmall`.

- **[critical]** The destructive button label (`text-primary` white on `health-critical`) is 3.76:1 and fails 1.4.3 on `Delete Account` (Flow 4, the Play-compliance path) and `Delete Racquet`. It also contradicts the PRD's "high-contrast confirmation modal" (prd.md line 84). (DESIGN.md frontmatter `components.button-destructive.text`, § Components → Confirmation Dialog.) *Fix (in palette):* set `button-destructive.text` to `{colors.on-primary}` (#000000, 5.58:1), or add an `on-destructive` alias pointing at the same value. Dark text on light red matches the M3 dark-scheme `onError` convention. *Fix (token change):* add a separate `destructive` #DC2626 with white text (4.83:1, fill 3.08:1 against the dialog). That keeps `health-critical` for arcs and borders and decouples the two meanings the Colors section already worries about.

### High

- **[high]** No real focus indicator exists. Keyboard focus shares the Level 2 hover treatment: a 12% Volt glow (1.36–1.68:1) plus a `surface` → `surface-elevated` shift (1.13:1). That fails 2.4.7 and 1.4.11, and focus can't be told apart from hover. No focus token exists. (DESIGN.md § Elevation & Depth Level 2; EXPERIENCE.md § Interaction Primitives "Hover (web)", § Accessibility Floor.) *Fix (in palette):* add a `focus-ring` component token to DESIGN frontmatter (2px solid `{colors.primary}`, 2px offset, radius following the component; 11.33–13.97:1 on every surface). On `primary`-filled controls, the offset puts the ring on the background, or use a `text-primary` ring. Split Level 2 into "Hover (web): glow" and "Focus (keyboard): focus-ring". Add to the Accessibility Floor: "every focusable element shows `focus-ring` on keyboard focus; hover styling never substitutes for it". Text fields keep the M3 focused border in `primary`.

- **[high]** The single-character shortcuts `L` and `N` fail 2.1.4 Character Key Shortcuts (Level A). They are global at expanded width, with no way to turn them off or remap them, and they don't depend on focus. Speech-input users and stray keypresses will open sheets. The spine also doesn't say they are suppressed while a text field has focus, and at 200% browser zoom the layout drops to medium width, so the shortcuts silently vanish. (EXPERIENCE.md § Interaction Primitives "Keyboard (web, expanded width)", § Information Architecture → Settings.) *Fix:* scope the shortcuts to "bag route on top, no modal open, no editable field focused". Add a Settings row `Keyboard shortcuts` (on/off, default on) to the IA tree, or use modifier combos (`Alt+Shift+L`, `Alt+Shift+N`; avoid the browser-reserved `Ctrl+L` / `Ctrl+N`). Show the shortcut in the FAB and Add Racquet tooltips so it can be found. `Esc` is not a character key and can stay.

- **[high]** Important failure messages exist only in a ~4 s auto-dismissing snackbar. The late write rejection arrives after the fact (Flow 1 ends with Marco pocketing his phone), and the card then silently reverts, so the user never learns the session was lost. Re-authentication failures and `Couldn't delete your account…` also disappear before slower readers, 200%-text users or magnifier users can read them (2.2.1 Timing Adjustable; 3.3.1 Error Identification). Flutter keeps action snackbars open only while TalkBack is on (`accessibleNavigation`), which doesn't cover sighted users. (EXPERIENCE.md § Component Patterns → Snackbar; § State Patterns → Late write rejection, Re-authentication cancelled / other account, Deletion failed.) *Fix:* split the Snackbar rule. Confirmations (`1h logged…`, `Fresh stringbed mounted!…`) stay at ~4 s, because the same information is visible on the card. Rejection and failure snackbars persist until dismissed, with a `Dismiss` action (or `Retry` for deletion). Rejection copy names what was lost (`Couldn't save your 1.5h session for Wilson Blade 98 — it was deleted on another device.`). Also show deletion failures as an inline message under `[Delete Account & All Data]`, matching the sign-in failure pattern.

- **[high]** Text-field and outline boundaries are invisible. The spines define no input border. The shipped theme maps `colorScheme.outline` to `surface-border`, so M3 outlined text fields, outlined chips and unselected chip borders come out at 1.11–1.37:1. The Add Racquet form fields (Flow 2 onboarding) and unselected duration/reason chips then fail 1.4.11 component identification. (DESIGN.md § Colors (`surface-border` "1px card outlines, dividers"), frontmatter `components.duration-chip.border`; § Shapes "text fields".) *Fix (in palette):* use `text-secondary` (5.62–7.71:1) for text-field borders and unselected chip outlines. Keep `surface-border` for decorative dividers, the gauge track and card outlines only. *Fix (new token):* add `outline: '#6B767B'` (3.19–3.93:1) to DESIGN frontmatter for interactive boundaries and map `colorScheme.outline` to it.

- **[high]** Racquet card actions have no specified accessible names or structure. The quick-log action is never given a visible label or semantics name (if it's icon-only, that fails 1.1.1/4.1.2). `[Restrung Today]` repeats on every card, so a screen-reader button list hears "Restrung Today" N times with no racquet context (2.4.6). A tappable card containing buttons produces nested interactive semantics on Flutter web (a button inside a button). (EXPERIENCE.md § Component Patterns → Racquet Card, § Accessibility Floor; DESIGN.md § Components → Racquet Card "Right".) *Fix:* in DESIGN Racquet Card, name the quick-log action (`Log` with an icon, or icon plus tooltip `Log session`). In the EXPERIENCE Accessibility Floor, add: "each card is one semantics container: an `Open details` node that merges name, string setup and the gauge label, followed by sibling buttons `Log session for {racquet}` and `Restrung today: {racquet}`; card buttons are never children of the card's tap target".

- **[high]** Flutter web semantics are off by default. Without opting in, screen-reader users must first find Flutter's hidden "Enable accessibility" placeholder before anything is exposed. The spines also don't mention per-route page titles (2.4.2, Level A) or the document language (3.1.1, Level A). (EXPERIENCE.md § Responsive & Platform → Web bullet.) *Fix:* add to the Web bullet: "On web, the app calls `SemanticsBinding.instance.ensureSemantics()` at startup; every route sets a `Title` (`My Virtual Bag — String Theory`, `Wilson Blade 98 — String Theory`, …); `index.html` declares `lang="en"`." Verify with NVDA+Chrome and VoiceOver+Safari as part of the web release checklist.

- **[high]** The card and gauge layout can't meet the stated 200% text-scale rule. The card is a three-zone horizontal row (gauge, text, two actions), and the gauge is a fixed ring with `metric-hero` 28px (56px at 200%) above a `HEALTH` micro-label. At compact width this clips or overflows on the core bag screen (Flows 1 and 3, 1.4.4), and no reflow behaviour is defined. (DESIGN.md § Components → Racquet Card, Radial Health Gauge; EXPERIENCE.md § Accessibility Floor "up to 200% without clipping".) *Fix:* add a large-text rule to DESIGN Racquet Card. At text scale ≥ 1.3, or when the card is narrower than its content, the actions move to a full-width row under the content, the racquet name wraps (never ellipsized) and the gauge diameter scales with the text scaler. Add an Accessibility Floor line naming the surfaces checked at 200%: bag card, gauge, Quick-Log sheet, Restrung Today sheet, destructive dialog, bottom navigation labels, snackbar.

### Medium

- **[medium]** Health tiers are only partly independent of color. EXPERIENCE requires `✓ Optimal`, `~ Caution`, `⚠ Restring`, but the DESIGN card spec shows only the critical `⚠ Restring` badge, so the 40–69 tier in Flow 1 (76% green → 67% amber) is signalled by color and number alone. Tier names also drift: FR-10 says Optimal/Degraded/Dead, the labels say Caution/Restring, and DESIGN's Colors table says "caution state". The gauge semantics label omits the tier, and TalkBack reads `~` as "tilde". (DESIGN.md § Components → Racquet Card, Radial Health Gauge; EXPERIENCE.md § Accessibility Floor, § Component Patterns → Radial Health Gauge.) *Fix:* in DESIGN, show a tier label on every card and on Racquet Detail, in its tier color (passes on `surface` and `health-critical-bg`, except `health-critical` text on `surface`; see the next finding). Reconcile the label set with FR-10 in one place. Extend the gauge label: `…string health 67 percent, Caution, …`. Specify that semantics labels drop the `✓ ~ ⚠` glyphs.

- **[medium]** Critical and error text color is unspecified. `health-critical` as text fails on `surface` (4.46:1) and `surface-elevated` (3.95:1), and the M3 default `error` (#CF6679, not overridden in the shipped theme) fails on `surface-elevated` (4.13:1). Inline form errors in the expanded-width Add Racquet dialog and any critical tier label inside a `surface` container would fail 1.4.3. (DESIGN.md § Colors.) *Fix (in palette):* allow `health-critical` as text only on `background` and `health-critical-bg`. *Fix (new token):* add `health-critical-text: '#F87171'` (5.38–6.27:1) for error and critical text on any surface, and map `colorScheme.error` to it.

- **[medium]** Selected state for chips and the racquet selector relies on fill or outline only. Their semantics are unspecified, and the reason chips and Sessions filter chips have no selected-state visual at all. The `[ASSUMPTION]` primary outline has no stated thickness. The horizontal racquet selector hides off-screen racquets, which is worse at 200%. `1.5h` is read literally. (DESIGN.md § Components → Quick-Log Sheet, Restrung Today Sheet; EXPERIENCE.md § Accessibility Floor.) *Fix:* in DESIGN, use the M3 `ChoiceChip` with its check icon for every single-select row (duration, reason, Sessions filter). The racquet selector gets a 2px `primary` outline plus a check icon, and wraps to multiple lines instead of scrolling horizontally. In the EXPERIENCE Accessibility Floor, add: chips expose `selected` and `inMutuallyExclusiveGroup`; duration chips have semantics labels `30 minutes`, `1 hour`, `1 and a half hours`, `2 hours`; the 48dp target applies to each chip; chip rows `Wrap`.

- **[medium]** Live stream updates have no screen-reader rule. Gauges animate on every snapshot (other device, app resume). If the semantics label is bound to the animated value, TalkBack re-reads the focused card through the tween, and cross-device changes may or may not announce. (EXPERIENCE.md § Component Patterns → Radial Health Gauge, Bag sync summary; § Accessibility Floor.) *Fix:* add to the Accessibility Floor: "Gauge semantics use the settled target value, never the animated value. Cards, gauges and the bag sync summary are not live regions. Only user-initiated outcomes are announced (via the snackbar). The bag sync summary is part of the bag header's semantics so it can be read on demand."

- **[medium]** Focus return is unspecified. The spines don't say where keyboard or accessibility focus goes after a sheet or dialog closes, after Racquet Detail closes on delete (the card is gone), after account deletion (new route), or when the Add Racquet form auto-opens and is dismissed on first sign-in. (EXPERIENCE.md § Accessibility Floor; § State Patterns → First sign-in, Racquet deleted, Deletion succeeded.) *Fix:* add a focus-destination list. Sheets and dialogs return focus to their trigger (FAB, card button, app-bar action). After a racquet is deleted, focus goes to the bag header. After account deletion, focus goes to the welcome headline, with the deletion message announced. A dismissed auto-opened Add Racquet form moves focus to `[Add Your First Racquet]`. The auto-opened form announces its title on open.

- **[medium]** The destructive dialog's "focus to Cancel first" can make screen readers skip the consequence text. If accessibility focus lands on `Cancel`, TalkBack users may never hear `This permanently deletes…`. (EXPERIENCE.md § Accessibility Floor, § Component Patterns → Destructive confirmation dialog.) *Fix:* separate the two kinds of focus. Keyboard focus defaults to `Cancel` (safe default, and `Esc` = Cancel). Screen-reader reading order starts at the dialog title, which names the route, then body, Cancel, confirm. Also say in the Delete Account body that Google may ask to confirm the account next, so the re-auth sheet isn't a surprise.

- **[medium]** Auth and deletion state messages have no announcement rule. The inline `Couldn't sign in…`, the popup-blocked message and `Your account and all data were deleted.` (shown "once") aren't specified as announced. On web, disabling the Google button while it has focus can drop focus to the document. If the web build uses Google's GIS-rendered button, it lives in an iframe platform view whose keyboard and screen-reader behaviour Flutter doesn't control. (EXPERIENCE.md § State Patterns → Sign-in failed, Web popup blocked, Deletion succeeded; § Component Patterns → Google Sign-In button.) *Fix:* inline auth messages are polite live regions and stay visible until the next sign-in attempt. "Shows once" means it stays on screen for that visit and is not a toast. The progress indicator has the semantics label `Signing in`. Focus stays on the button while it's busy. Add a web verification item for the button: Tab reaches it, Enter/Space activates it, and it's announced as "Sign in with Google, button".

- **[medium]** Sheets have no visible close control. Dismissal is swipe, scrim tap or `Esc`. The swipe is a dragging movement whose scrim alternative isn't obvious (2.5.7), TalkBack users get no visible exit, and at 200% the full-width `Save Session` / `Save & Reset` can scroll out of view, especially once `[Change Strings]` expands with the keyboard open. (DESIGN.md § Components → Quick-Log Sheet, Restrung Today Sheet; EXPERIENCE.md § Interaction Primitives, § Accessibility Floor "Sheets scroll".) *Fix:* add a 48dp `Close` icon button (semantics `Close`) in each sheet header. In EXPERIENCE, add: "sheets scroll with the keyboard inset; the primary action stays pinned at the bottom of the sheet".

- **[medium]** Form error behaviour is too thin for 3.3.1–3.3.3. "Out-of-range values are blocked inline" doesn't say the error is text, names the allowed range, or is announced. How "required" is shown is unspecified. The silent auto-refill of Max court hours when the material changes isn't explained. (EXPERIENCE.md § Component Patterns → Add Racquet form.) *Fix:* add that errors appear as text under the field, name the range (`Tension must be between {min} and {max} lbs`) and are announced. On a failed save, focus moves to the first invalid field. Required fields say `(required)` in the label text. Max court hours shows the helper text `Suggested for Monofilament`.

### Low

- **[low]** Card outlines (1.26–1.37:1), the card/background step (1.09:1) and the gauge track (1.26:1) are below 3:1. They aren't strictly required, because card content and the % value identify the card and its state, but low-vision users lose the card's tap area in the 2–3 column grids. (DESIGN.md § Elevation & Depth Level 1, § Components → Radial Health Gauge.) *Fix:* optional. If the `outline` token from the high finding is adopted, consider it for card borders at medium and expanded widths. Otherwise leave as is.

- **[low]** Motion: the reduce-motion `[ASSUMPTION]` is right but covers only the gauge. The skeleton shimmer `[ASSUMPTION]` can run past 5 s on a slow first load (2.2.2). No rule forbids a future pulsing critical badge. 2.3.3 is AAA and so not required. (EXPERIENCE.md § Accessibility Floor, § State Patterns → First snapshot loading; DESIGN.md § Do's and Don'ts "animate gauge… at 60 fps".) *Fix:* adopt the assumption. Extend it to sheet and dialog transitions, the snackbar slide, the gauge color tween and the shimmer (static skeleton under `MediaQuery.disableAnimations`). Stop the shimmer after 5 s in all cases. Give the skeleton the semantics label `Loading your bag`. Add a Don't: "no pulsing, blinking or looping animation on cards or badges". Verify that Flutter web honours `prefers-reduced-motion`; if it doesn't, add a Settings toggle.

- **[low]** Screen titles and section labels have no heading semantics, so screen-reader heading navigation (1.3.1, 2.4.6) has nothing to land on on the bag, detail and settings screens. (EXPERIENCE.md § Accessibility Floor.) *Fix:* add "screen titles (`display`), sheet and dialog titles and settings group labels (`section`) are marked as headers".

- **[low]** Smaller 200% risks: M3 navigation bar labels, multi-line rejection snackbars and the restring timeline row (`Aug 1 – Oct 2 • Luxilon ALU Power • 19.2 court hours • 62 days`) will wrap heavily. The timeline row also reads as one undifferentiated string. (EXPERIENCE.md § Component Patterns → Restring history timeline; § Responsive & Platform.) *Fix:* allow timeline entries to wrap into two lines (dates / string / hours and days). Give each entry a semantics label in full words (`August 1 to October 2, Luxilon ALU Power, 19.2 court hours, 62 days`).

- **[low]** The "Sign-out with pending writes" open question has a cognitive-safety answer: a silent sign-out with queued writes risks unseen data loss on shared devices. (EXPERIENCE.md § Open Questions.) *Fix:* recommend a confirmation listing the count (`2 changes are still waiting to sync. Sign out anyway?`, with `Stay signed in` / `Sign out`).

- **[low]** The PRD has no accessibility NFR, so the WCAG 2.2 AA target isn't traceable to acceptance criteria. The spines can't fix that on their own. (prd.md, NFR section; EXPERIENCE.md § Accessibility Floor.) *Fix:* add one line to the Accessibility Floor ("Target: WCAG 2.2 AA on Android and web; TalkBack and NVDA/VoiceOver verified on Flows 1–4"), and raise a PRD NFR through the PM.

- **[low]** Flutter web renders text to canvas, so user text-spacing overrides (1.4.12) and user stylesheets don't apply. This is a platform limitation and not fixable in the spine. (EXPERIENCE.md § Responsive & Platform → Web.) *Fix:* record it as a known limitation in the Web bullet, and rely on the 200% text-scale and browser-zoom (1.4.4/1.4.10) behaviour instead.
