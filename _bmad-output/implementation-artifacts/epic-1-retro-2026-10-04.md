## Findings

### Aggregate Views

1. **Architecture Delta & Module Boundaries** (Source: `string_theory_server`, `string_theory_client`, `string_theory_flutter`):
   - *Observation:* Clean layer separation between Serverpod PostgreSQL ORM backend, auto-generated protocol client, and Flutter client. Multi-tenancy is enforced on all mutations and queries via `_resolveUserId(session)` in `BagEndpoint.dart:9-36`.
   - *Disposition:* Accept as-is. Architecture matches `ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md` Section 3.

2. **God-Class & Component Growth** (Source: `string_theory_flutter/lib/screens/virtual_bag_screen.dart:1-722`):
   - *Observation:* `VirtualBagScreen` grew to ~720 lines because it houses `VirtualBagScreen`, `_VirtualBagScreenState`, `RacquetCard`, and `_buildHealthGauge`.
   - *Disposition:* Fix in Epic 2. Story 2.2 ("Animated Radial Health Gauge Widget & Racquet Card Integration") is already scoped to refactor `RacquetCard` and extract `RadialHealthGauge` into dedicated files under `lib/widgets/`.

3. **Pattern Divergence & Backend Initial Court Hours** (Source: `string_theory_server/lib/src/endpoints/bag_endpoint.dart:125-126`, `string_theory_flutter/lib/bloc/bag_bloc.dart:138-165`):
   - *Observation:* `BagEndpoint.createRacquet` hardcoded `totalCourtHours: 0.0` and `currentHealthScore: 100`. In Story 1.5, `BagBloc._onSeedSampleBag` mapped the seeded racquets to display realistic demo hours (5h, 9h, 25h) and health scores (76%, 58%, 34%).
   - *Disposition:* Defer to Epic 2. In Story 2.1 (`DecayEngine`) and Story 2.3 (`DailyDecayFutureCall`), the authoritative recalculation logic will be implemented on the backend and persist dynamic tension decay.

4. **Duplication Map** (Source: `string_theory_flutter/lib/theme/court_midnight_theme.dart:1-120`):
   - *Observation:* Centralized Court Midnight palette tokens (`#121516` canvas, `#1A1E20` surface, `#2A3034` borders, `#C6F10E` electric volt) were consistently reused across modals, forms, cards, and theme extensions without drift.
   - *Disposition:* Accept as-is.

### Diff Lenses (Adversarial, Edge Cases, Verification Gaps)

1. **Unstrung Racquet Representation** (Source: `virtual_bag_screen.dart:488-510`, `virtual_bag_screen_test.dart:313-345`):
   - *Review finding:* Without an active string job, `RacquetCard` previously defaulted health to 100% green.
   - *Resolution:* Patched in Story 1.5 review cycle. Displays `'--'`, `'UNSTRUNG'`, and neutral tertiary color. Verified with automated widget test.

2. **Tablet Grid Aspect Ratio Overflows** (Source: `virtual_bag_screen.dart:272-278`):
   - *Review finding:* A fixed aspect ratio (`2.3`) caused `RenderFlex` overflow on ~600–700dp viewports when critical health warning banners appeared.
   - *Resolution:* Patched using `mainAxisExtent: 140`, ensuring fixed, guaranteed height across all tablet screen sizes.

3. **Accidental Racquet Deletion** (Source: `virtual_bag_screen.dart:643-690`):
   - *Review finding:* Tapping 'Delete Racquet' previously deleted the frame immediately.
   - *Resolution:* Added confirmation `AlertDialog` modal before dispatching `DeleteRacquet`.

4. **Verification Coverage Gaps** (Source: `widget_test.dart`, `virtual_bag_screen_test.dart`, `bag_bloc_test.dart`):
   - *Review finding:* Missing integration assertions for root body mounting, `BagLoading`, `BagError` recovery, and RPC failure.
   - *Resolution:* Added 4 new automated test cases across Flutter test suites; suite grew from 36 to 40 tests, all passing green.
