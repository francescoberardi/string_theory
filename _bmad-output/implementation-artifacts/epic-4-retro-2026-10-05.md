### Code Lenses & Hardening

1. **Pessimistic Lock on Concurrency Race (Story 4.1):**
   - *Finding:* Simultaneous `restringRacquet` calls (e.g. rapid double-tap on UI) could both read `oldJob.isActive == true` before either updated, resulting in duplicate `RestringEvent` rows and dual active jobs.
   - *Disposition:* Hardened in commit `76ec583`. Added `LockMode.forUpdate` on the racquet row inside the transaction.
2. **Hardening Test-Only Endpoint Knobs (Story 4.1):**
   - *Finding:* `simulateFailure` and `restringDate` parameters on `restringRacquet` were accessible without run-mode checks.
   - *Disposition:* Hardened in commit `76ec583`. Moved run-mode checks to reject `simulateFailure` and non-null `restringDate` in production with 400 `BagException`.
3. **Rollback & Negative Path Test Assertions (Story 4.1):**
   - *Finding:* Rollback test originally caught general `Exception` rather than verifying specific exception details, row counts, and stream silence.
   - *Disposition:* Patched in commit `d1c8b68`. Added explicit checks that no new `StringJob` exists and no `restrung` stream message is published on rollback.
4. **Contrast Fix for Restrung Toast (Story 4.2):**
   - *Finding:* White text on `healthOptimal` (#10B981) background had a low contrast ratio (~2.5:1).
   - *Disposition:* Patched in commit `76304d7`. Styled toast with black text and icon, matching the high-contrast `primaryVolt` badge pattern.
5. **Accidental Max Hours Reset on Category Click (Story 4.2):**
   - *Finding:* Tapping the already-selected category chip in `RestringSheet` re-triggered default hours assignment, discarding player customizations.
   - *Disposition:* Patched in commit `76304d7`. Guarded with `if (category != _category)`.
6. **Pull-to-Refresh Gesture Cancellation (Story 4.3):**
   - *Finding:* Setting `_isLoadingHistory = true` during refresh unmounted the `ListView`, interrupting the pull-to-refresh physics.
   - *Disposition:* Patched in commit `e23241f`. Maintained `ListView` mounted during background refresh.
